# Delivery outcomes

- [x] The single-page portfolio clearly presents Manisha Dhar as a Biology teacher and career guide for NEET aspirants and post-NEET students.
- [x] The site uses an original warm, science-led editorial identity inspired by the reference’s sparse black-and-cream typography, oversized spaced headlines, horizontal narrative bands, and art-directed non-corporate character.
- [x] The hero introduces Manisha’s Biology teaching, NEET guidance, and post-NEET counselling expertise and pairs the introduction with an abstract biology-inspired visual motif.
- [x] The teaching philosophy section expresses a warm, science-led, learner-centred approach through editorial storytelling and strong typography.
- [x] The Biology and NEET support section explains how Manisha helps aspirants understand concepts, prepare strategically, and build confidence.
- [x] The post-NEET counselling section explains guidance for evaluating options and navigating next steps after results.
- [x] Guidance themes are presented as an accessible responsive horizontal content band with a graceful mobile interaction.
- [x] The learner-notes section uses concise anonymised reflections without fabricated personal details.
- [x] Contact and booking calls to action appear at key points and include a practical enquiry pathway that opens a ready-to-send email draft.
- [x] The experience is responsive, semantic, accessible, keyboard-friendly, readable at good contrast, and respectful of reduced-motion preferences.
- [x] The page uses abstract cellular, botanical, or microscopic forms instead of an invented photorealistic portrait.
